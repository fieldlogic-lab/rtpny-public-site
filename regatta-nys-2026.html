NYS Championships 2026.

April 17–18, 2026 · Saratoga Springs, New York. A practical race-weekend guide distilled from the team itinerary.

Use this page as a reference. Race times, parking instructions, lodging, and transportation can change. The coach or current team notice is the final word.
When

Friday + Saturday

Friday is the travel, trailer, and preparation day. Saturday begins with early racing and a deliberate warm-up. Finals were listed as event-dependent in the original itinerary.

Where

Saratoga Springs area

The team lodging was in the Queensbury area. The race venue used a trailer-side parking/drop-off approach near Union Avenue and the bridge; follow the current event map when issued.

Race morning

First starts: 9:16 a.m.

The itinerary listed starts at 9:16, 9:24, 10:28, and 10:44 a.m. Finals were to be posted after the morning results.

Team rhythm

Arrive ready to work

Expect trailer setup, launch preparation, warm-up, racing, recovery, equipment reset, and a short debrief. Keep the athlete area clear and follow coxswain and coach calls.

Pack

Race uniform or racing shirt, spandex or compression bottoms, warm layers, rain gear, dry clothes, slides or water shoes, sneakers, sunscreen, sunglasses, water, and snacks.

Plan for change

Saratoga weather can shift quickly. Bring enough layers to stay warm before rowing and a dry change for after racing without overloading the vehicle.
